Quick Glossary
Lux (lx)
Lumens per square metre. How much light falls on a surface. A dining room needs 300–400 lux.
Lumens (lm)
Total light output of a fixture. A typical downlight outputs 700–1,200 lm.
CRI
Colour Rendering Index. How accurately light shows colours. CRI 90+ for hospitality and retail.
CCT / Kelvin (K)
Colour temperature — warm (2700K) to cool (5000K). 2700–3000K for most residential.
UGR
Unified Glare Rating. Lower is better. UGR ≤19 for office and task environments.
Room Index (k)
L×W ÷ H(L+W). Determines utilisation factor for lux calculations. Higher = more efficient.
