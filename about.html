"After 18 years leading Flos in India, I had seen something consistently: the people who needed European lighting the most, architects and designers working on the country's finest projects, had no proper platform to access, understand, and specify it."

Amanpreet Singh spent the better part of three decades inside the European lighting industry in India. As Country Head of Flos India for 18 years, he built relationships with architects and interior designers from Mumbai to Delhi to Bangalore, watching the design profession grow in sophistication and ambition.

What he observed, consistently, was a gap. The specification process for European lighting was fragmented, knowledge was hard to come by, and professionals were making decisions about some of the most visible elements of their projects without adequate tools or support.

"The Lightists exists to close that gap. We bring Europe's best lighting to India's best designers, with the knowledge and platform to use it well."

The Lightists is the result: a professional platform that combines a curated catalogue of 25+ European brands, specification and calculation tools built for architects and designers, a Design Academy with technical guides and trend reports, and a team with more lighting expertise than any other company in India.

We work across luxury residential, boutique hospitality, and high-end commercial projects, from intimate apartments to grand hotel lobbies. We are based in Delhi and Bangalore, and work pan India.
